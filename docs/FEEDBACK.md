# User Feedback — Level 5

## Feedback Collection Method

**Collection Channels:**
- **Google Forms:** Anonymous feedback form for structured responses
- **X (Twitter) DMs:** Direct messages from @DhruvaMandavkar
- **Discord/Telegram:** Midnight Builder Challenge community channels
- **GitHub Issues:** Technical feedback and bug reports
- **Email:** Direct contact through GitHub profile

**Feedback Form Questions:**
1. What did you try to do with ZKHire?
2. Was the privacy model clear? (Did you understand what stays private?)
3. How was the wallet connection experience?
4. What confused you or didn't work as expected?
5. What feature would make this more useful?
6. On a scale of 1-10, how likely are you to use this in real job applications?
7. Any other comments or suggestions?

**Target:** At least 10 detailed responses from 50 users
